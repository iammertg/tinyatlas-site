Tiny Atlas opens four worlds for everyone. This link opens the other eight at no cost, for good.

Open it on the iPhone or iPad

Open this page on the iPhone or iPad your child plays on. The link hands off to the App Store app, so on a computer it shows an empty page and nothing happens.

Open all worlds, free

How it goes

  1. Tap the button. The App Store opens with the offer and asks you to confirm. It costs nothing.
  2. If Tiny Atlas isn’t on the device yet, let the App Store install it. If it doesn’t offer to, install Tiny Atlas with the button below first, then tap the one above again.
  3. Open Tiny Atlas. The padlocks are gone. If one is still there, open the grown-ups’ area and tap “Restore Purchases”.

The small print

  • One redemption per Apple Account.
  • There is a fixed number of them. When they run out, the link stops working.
  • The offer is Apple’s own, redeemed through the App Store. Tiny Atlas never sees anything about your account or your payment details.
