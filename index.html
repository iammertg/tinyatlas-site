Tiny Atlas

Twelve little hand-drawn worlds to tap, drag and discover. For children from 2 to 6.

Coming soon to theApp Store

A picture book that plays back

Twelve little worlds wait behind small round windows: a pond, a farm, a kitchen, a snowy evening, a reef, a garden, a music room, a painting studio, a busy street, outer space, a dinosaur valley and a board of switches and gears. Open one, and everything that looks touchable answers. The frog jumps, the bells ring, the snowman gets its hat.

There are no words to read and nothing to win or lose. No timers, no scores. Gentle sounds play only when your child plays, and the silent switch is respected.

Inside the windows

For grown-ups

  • Four worlds are free

    The pond, the farm, the kitchen and winter, to play for as long as you like.

  • One purchase opens the rest

    A single purchase, once, opens the other eight worlds. With Family Sharing they open on your family’s devices too. No subscription, no account.

  • No ads, no data collected

    No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics. What your child does stays on the device.

  • A discovery journal

    In plain sentences, it tells you what your child has found in the worlds and suggests something to ask them about. No scores, no comparisons.

  • Made for small hands

    Tap and drag are all it takes, and many fingers at once are welcome. Everything answers right away.

  • A gate for grown-ups

    Purchases and links sit behind a grown-ups’ gate. A locked world only gives a little shake: your child never sees a price.
