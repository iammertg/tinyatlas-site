Tiny Atlas, called Minik Atlas in Turkish

Tiny Atlas is a calm, wordless play app for children aged 2 to 6. Twelve hand-drawn worlds wait behind small round windows, from a pond and a reef to a snowy evening and a dinosaur valley, and everything that looks touchable answers to a tap or a drag. There is nothing to win or lose, no timers and no scores. Four worlds are free, and one purchase opens the other eight for the whole family. There are no ads and no data is collected. For grown-ups, a discovery journal tells in plain sentences what their child has found.
